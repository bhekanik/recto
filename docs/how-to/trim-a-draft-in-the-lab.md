# Trim a draft in the Lab

The Lab reads a library draft and proposes cuts. It only deletes; it never rewrites, adds or reorders your words. AI must be on (`Toggle AI` in the command palette), and the draft must be synced.

Open the Lab with the flask button beside the formatting toolbar, or search for "Lab" in the command palette, and choose how hard to trim:

- Slight trim: about 10% shorter
- Tighten more: about 20% shorter
- Even sharper: about 30% shorter
- Cut in half: about 50% shorter

The percentage is a request, not a quota. The Lab cuts what contributes least (repetition, throat-clearing, hedges and filler) and stops early rather than cut meaning, facts, quotations or your voice.

While it reads the draft, the bar under the draft says so. When it finishes, the words it would cut fade in place and the bar shows the word count before and after. Click a faded word to keep it; click it again to cut it after all. `Walk through` steps through the cuts one at a time with the Lab's reason for each, and `Keep the words` or `Cut them` decides each one. `Make the cuts` deletes what is still faded as one step, so a single Undo brings it all back. `Done` sets the proposal aside without changing anything.

The proposal belongs to the text it was made for. If you type while it is showing, it goes away; run the Lab again for the new text. A suggestion the Lab could not match to exactly one place in the draft is left out and counted in the bar, so a cut never lands on the wrong words.
