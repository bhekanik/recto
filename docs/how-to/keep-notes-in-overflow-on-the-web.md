# Keep notes in Overflow on the web

Overflow is the scratchpad beside a draft: spare paragraphs, links and outlines that are not part of the text. On the web, choose `Toggle Overflow` in the command palette to open it. It is the same Overflow the Mac app shows for the same draft, and edits on either sync to the other.

Type or paste into the panel; it saves shortly after you stop typing. If the notes changed on another device while you were editing, Overflow keeps both copies until you choose: `View other copy`, then `Keep this copy` or `Use other copy`.

To set a passage aside, select it in Rich, Raw or Vim and choose `Stash selection in Overflow`, or press Control-Shift-X on a Mac (Alt+Shift+X elsewhere). The passage joins the end of Overflow, then leaves the draft. To bring text back, put the draft's caret where it belongs, select the text in Overflow and press Ctrl+Return.

Each move saves its destination before it changes its source, so if something fails halfway the text is in both places, never in neither. Undo in the draft reverses the draft half of a move. Unlike the Mac app, the web does not also take the text back out of Overflow, so after undoing a stash on the web the passage is in both places; delete the copy you do not want.

Overflow belongs to drafts you own. It is not shown for documents shared with you.
